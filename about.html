The Agora Narrative

A debate society named after the public square must act like one.

The ancient agora was more than a marketplace; it was a civic engine for exchange, argument, and presence. Agora at ABESEC carries this torch into the modern age, transforming technical students into articulate leaders of tomorrow.

The Institutional Core

More than a club. A culture.

We reject the notion that a debating society is merely a collection of events. Agora is a continuous intellectual conversation—a room that remains active even when the lights are off.

  • Institutional authority over transient activity.
  • A commitment to the rigors of the BP and Conventional formats.
  • Building a durable record of ABESEC’s intellectual heritage.
