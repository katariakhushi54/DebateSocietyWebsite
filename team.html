The Executive Council

Leadership is the stewardship of a legacy.

The stewards of Agora are more than organizers; they are the guardians of the Square’s intellectual integrity. We lead by example, mentoring the next generation of orators and ensuring that every round meets the highest standards of the circuit.

The Mandate of the Council
  • Upholding the ceremonial logic of the House.
  • Mentoring novices through the transformation cycle.
  • Curating a motion bank of contemporary relevance.
  • Representing ABESEC with honor on the national stage.
  • Ensuring the continuity of the Agora legacy.
